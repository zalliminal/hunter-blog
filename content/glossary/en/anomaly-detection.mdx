## What is Anomaly Detection?

In one sentence: Anomaly detection is finding the unusual needle in the haystack of normal system activity.

Anomaly detection uses statistical analysis, machine learning, or rule-based systems to identify patterns that deviate from normal behavior. In security, this means detecting things like a user logging in at 3 AM from an unusual country, a sudden spike in outbound traffic, or a process making unexpected network connections.

It's a core capability of SOC tools, SIEM systems, and EDR platforms. Anomaly detection is exactly what Hugging Face used to spot unusual activity in their infrastructure.
