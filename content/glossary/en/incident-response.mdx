## What is Incident Response?

In one sentence: Incident response is the playbook your team follows when something bad happens in your systems.

Incident Response (IR) is a structured approach to handling security breaches. It typically follows phases: Preparation, Identification, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, and Lessons Learned. A good IR plan minimizes damage, reduces recovery time, and helps prevent future incidents.

IR teams (often within a SOC) work around the clock to detect, analyze, and respond to threats. Digital forensics is closely related — it's the investigation that happens as part of IR to understand exactly what occurred.
