## What is an APT?

In one sentence: An APT is a highly skilled, well-funded attack group that patiently breaks into networks and stays hidden for months or years.

Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs) are typically state-sponsored groups that conduct long-term, targeted cyber espionage campaigns. Unlike common criminals who want quick payouts, APTs are methodical — they research targets, use custom malware, move laterally, and exfiltrate data slowly to avoid detection.

Famous APT groups include APT29 (Cozy Bear), APT28 (Fancy Bear), and APT1. They are the most challenging adversaries for any security team.
