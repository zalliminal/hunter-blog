## What is a Threat Actor?

In one sentence: A threat actor is whoever is behind a cyberattack — a person, group, or nation-state.

Threat actors are categorized by their motivation and resources: script kiddies (low-skill attackers), hacktivists (politically motivated), cybercriminals (financially motivated), insider threats (employees), and APT groups (state-sponsored, highly sophisticated). Understanding the threat actor helps predict their tactics and goals.
