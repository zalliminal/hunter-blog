## What is C2?

In one sentence: C2 is the attacker's remote control channel to command compromised systems.

Command and Control (C2, also C&C) infrastructure allows attackers to send instructions to and receive data from compromised systems. This can be a simple HTTP server, a complex peer-to-peer network, or communication hidden in legitimate services like social media.

C2 communication is a key stage in the kill chain. Detecting and disrupting C2 channels is a primary goal of network defense — if you cut the C2, the attacker loses control of their foothold.
