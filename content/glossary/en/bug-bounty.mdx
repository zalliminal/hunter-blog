## What is a Bug Bounty?

In one sentence: Bug bounty programs pay ethical hackers to find and report security flaws instead of exploiting them.

Bug bounty programs are organized by companies (like Google, Meta, Microsoft) and platforms (HackerOne, Bugcrowd) to incentivize security researchers to responsibly report vulnerabilities. Rewards range from a few hundred dollars for low-severity issues to millions for critical zero-day exploits.

Bug bounties have become a standard part of security strategy. They give companies access to thousands of skilled researchers who find bugs that internal teams might miss.
