## What is a Guardrail in AI?

In one sentence: Guardrails are the rules and filters that keep an AI from saying or doing something harmful.

In the context of AI, guardrails are safety mechanisms designed to constrain model behavior. They prevent LLMs from generating harmful content, leaking sensitive information, or acting outside their intended scope. Guardrails can be implemented as moderation filters, output validation, prompt-level instructions, or external monitoring systems.

For example, a guardrail might block an LLM from answering questions about creating weapons, or ensure it refuses to generate code that would be used maliciously. Guardrails are a critical part of responsible AI deployment.
