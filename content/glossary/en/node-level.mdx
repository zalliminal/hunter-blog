## What is Node-Level?

In one sentence: Node-level refers to anything that belongs to or happens on a single machine (node) in a distributed system.

In distributed systems and clustering, a "node" is a single machine or instance. "Node-level" describes operations, configurations, or resources that are scoped to that individual node rather than the entire cluster. For example, node-level storage means data stored locally on that machine, while cluster-level storage would be shared across all nodes.

Understanding the node-level vs cluster-level distinction is important for designing resilient distributed systems.
