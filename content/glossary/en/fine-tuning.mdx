## What is Fine-Tuning?

In one sentence: Fine-tuning is giving a pre-trained model extra practice on specific data to make it better at a particular job.

Fine-tuning takes a pre-trained model (like an LLM) and continues training it on a smaller, task-specific dataset. This adapts the model's behavior — it can learn new skills, adopt a specific tone, or gain domain knowledge without being trained from scratch.

For example, you might fine-tune a general LLM on customer support conversations to make it a better support chatbot. Fine-tuning is also a vector for model poisoning if the training data is compromised.
