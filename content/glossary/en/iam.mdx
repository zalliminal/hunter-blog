## What is IAM?

In one sentence: IAM is who you are and what you're allowed to do in a system.

Identity and Access Management (IAM) is the framework of policies, processes, and technologies that manage digital identities and control access to resources. It answers two questions: "Who is this user?" (authentication) and "What are they allowed to do?" (authorization).

Cloud providers like AWS have extensive IAM systems where you define users, groups, roles, and policies. A single IAM misconfiguration can lead to massive data breaches.
