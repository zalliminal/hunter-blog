## What is SSO?

In one sentence: SSO lets you log in once and access everything without re-entering your password.

Single Sign-On (SSO) is an authentication scheme that allows users to log in with one set of credentials and access multiple applications or services without re-authenticating. It's implemented using protocols like SAML, OAuth, and OpenID Connect.

SSO improves user experience (fewer passwords to remember) and security (centralized access control, easier to revoke access). However, the identity provider becomes a single point of failure — if it's compromised, all connected services are at risk.
