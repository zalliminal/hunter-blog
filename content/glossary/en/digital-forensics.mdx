## What is Digital Forensics?

In one sentence: Digital forensics is the CSI-style investigation of computer systems to figure out how a breach happened.

Digital forensics involves collecting, preserving, and analyzing digital evidence after a security incident. Forensic analysts examine logs, disk images, memory dumps, network traffic, and system artifacts to reconstruct the timeline of events, identify the attack vector, and determine what data was accessed or stolen.

Forensic findings are often used for legal proceedings, insurance claims, and improving security posture. Chain of custody and evidence preservation are critical principles in forensic investigations.
