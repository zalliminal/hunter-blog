## What is Responsible Disclosure?

In one sentence: Responsible disclosure means telling the company about a vulnerability first, giving them time to fix it before telling the world.

Responsible disclosure (also called coordinated disclosure) is an ethical practice where security researchers privately notify affected vendors about vulnerabilities, allow them a reasonable time to develop and deploy a fix, and only then publicly disclose the details. Typical timelines range from 30 to 90 days.

This protects users from attacks while the fix is being developed. Full disclosure (immediate public release) is controversial and generally discouraged unless the vendor is unresponsive.
