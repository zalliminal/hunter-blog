## What is Credential Harvesting?

In one sentence: After getting into one system, attackers steal login credentials to break into more systems.

Credential harvesting is the process of collecting usernames, passwords, API keys, tokens, and certificates from a compromised system. Attackers look for credential files, browser-stored passwords, config files, environment variables, and memory dumps. These stolen credentials enable lateral movement and privilege escalation.

This is why credential hygiene — unique passwords, MFA, short-lived tokens — is so important. A single compromised credential can lead to a full network takeover.
