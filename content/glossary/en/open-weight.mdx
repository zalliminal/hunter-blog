## What is Open Weight?

In one sentence: Open-weight AI models are those where the actual trained brain (weights) is shared publicly.

In AI, "weights" are the parameters learned during training — they represent what the model knows. An open-weight model means these trained parameters are publicly released, so anyone can download the model, run it on their own hardware, fine-tune it for specific tasks, or inspect it.

Examples include Meta's Llama series, Mistral's models, and Stability AI's models. Open-weight models differ from closed models (like GPT-4 or Claude) which are only accessible through paid APIs and whose weights remain private.
