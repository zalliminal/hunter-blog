## What is a Classifier?

In one sentence: A classifier is a filter that checks whether something is safe or harmful before it gets shown to a user.

In AI safety, a classifier is a model or system trained to identify and block harmful content. It sits between the LLM and the user, analyzing the model's output and deciding whether it violates safety policies. If it does, the output is blocked or replaced with a safe response.

Classifiers can also be used on the input side to detect malicious prompts before they reach the model. They are a key component of the guardrail stack in production AI systems.
