## What is an AI Agent?

In one sentence: An AI agent is an autonomous system that perceives its environment, makes decisions, and takes actions to achieve goals without requiring human intervention at every step.

AI agents go beyond simple chatbots — they can use tools, browse the web, execute code, interact with APIs, and remember context across interactions. They combine an LLM with capabilities like planning, tool use, and memory to accomplish complex tasks autonomously.

Examples include coding agents, research assistants, and automated workflow systems. The safety and reliability of AI agents is an active area of research.
