## What is a Certificate?

In one sentence: A digital certificate is like an ID card for a website — it proves who the site really is.

An SSL/TLS certificate is a digital document issued by a Certificate Authority (CA) that binds a cryptographic key to an organization's or domain's identity. When you visit a secure site, the server presents its certificate to prove its identity, and your browser verifies it against trusted CAs.

Certificates are essential for HTTPS, email encryption, code signing, and mutual TLS authentication. Expired or invalid certificates trigger browser warnings.
