## What is a Jailbreak?

In one sentence: Jailbreaking an AI model means finding a creative way to make it ignore its safety rules.

In the context of LLMs, a jailbreak is a carefully crafted prompt or technique designed to bypass the model's built-in guardrails and safety filters. Unlike prompt injection (which overrides instructions), jailbreaks exploit loopholes in how the model was trained to behave.

Common techniques include role-playing scenarios ("You are DAN — Do Anything Now"), hypothetical reasoning, or encoding requests in ways the guardrails don't catch. Jailbreaking is an ongoing cat-and-mouse game between attackers and model developers.
