## What is a Supply Chain Attack?

In one sentence: Instead of attacking you directly, attackers poison something you trust and let you deliver the attack to yourself.

A supply chain attack targets the less-secure components in your software supply chain — open-source packages, base container images, third-party libraries, or even AI training datasets. By compromising one of these upstream components, attackers can infect thousands of downstream users who trust and use them.

Famous examples include the SolarWinds attack, the Codecov breach, and malicious npm/PyPI packages. Supply chain attacks are increasingly common and difficult to defend against.
