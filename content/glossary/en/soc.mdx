## What is a SOC?

In one sentence: A SOC is a team of security experts who watch your systems 24/7 and respond to threats in real-time.

A Security Operations Center (SOC) is a centralized unit responsible for continuously monitoring, detecting, analyzing, and responding to security incidents. SOC analysts use SIEM systems, EDR tools, and threat intelligence to identify threats, triage alerts, and coordinate incident response.

SOC teams typically operate in tiers: Tier 1 (triage alerts), Tier 2 (investigate), and Tier 3 (hunt threats and advanced analysis). Modern SOCs also incorporate SOAR (Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response) to automate repetitive tasks.
