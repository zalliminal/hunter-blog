## What is Hallucination?

In one sentence: When an AI model confidently tells you something that's completely made up.

Hallucination in AI refers to when an LLM generates information that sounds plausible and authoritative but is factually incorrect. The model isn't lying — it's just generating text that follows patterns in its training data without any understanding of truth.

For example, an LLM might confidently cite a court case that never existed, invent a research paper, or describe a person who doesn't exist. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is one technique used to reduce hallucinations by grounding responses in external data.
