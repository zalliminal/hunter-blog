## What is TLS/SSL?

In one sentence: TLS/SSL is the encryption technology that keeps your data private when it travels between your browser and a website.

TLS (Transport Layer Security) and its predecessor SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) are cryptographic protocols that provide secure communication over a network. They encrypt data in transit, verify server identity through certificates, and ensure data integrity. You know it's working when you see the padlock icon and `https://` in your browser.

SSL is deprecated and insecure — modern systems use TLS 1.2 or 1.3. It's the foundation of secure web communication, email security, and VPNs.
