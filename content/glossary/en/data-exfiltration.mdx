## What is Data Exfiltration?

In one sentence: Data exfiltration is stealing data from a system by sending it out without authorization.

Data exfiltration is the final stage of many cyberattacks where stolen data is transferred out of the victim's network. Attackers use various methods: compressing and uploading data via C2 channels, using encrypted tunnels, hiding data in DNS queries, or physically copying it to removable media.

Preventing exfiltration involves monitoring outbound traffic, data loss prevention (DLP) tools, and egress filtering. A successful exfiltration is often the primary objective of a targeted attack.
