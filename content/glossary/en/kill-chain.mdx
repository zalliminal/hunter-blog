## What is a Kill Chain?

In one sentence: The kill chain breaks down a cyberattack into stages, from initial probing to the final damage.

The cyber kill chain, originally developed by Lockheed Martin, describes the stages of a cyberattack: Reconnaissance → Weaponization → Delivery → Exploitation → Installation → Command & Control → Actions on Objectives. Understanding each stage helps defenders detect and disrupt attacks before they reach their goal.

Disrupting any single stage breaks the chain and stops the attack. This model is fundamental to threat intelligence and security operations.
