## What is an Indicator of Compromise?

In one sentence: An IoC is a forensic clue that tells you a system has been breached.

Indicators of Compromise (IoCs) are pieces of forensic evidence that suggest a security incident has occurred. Common IoCs include unusual IP addresses, file hashes of known malware, unexpected domain names in logs, registry changes, or unusual network traffic patterns.

IoCs are used by SOC teams, EDR systems, and threat intelligence platforms to detect and respond to breaches. They are a critical part of incident response and digital forensics.
