## What is Penetration Testing?

In one sentence: Penetration testing is hiring an ethical hacker to break into your systems so you can fix the holes before real attackers find them.

Penetration testing (pen testing) is a simulated cyberattack performed by security professionals to identify vulnerabilities in systems, networks, and applications. Unlike bug bounty (where many researchers hunt for any bug), pen testing is usually a focused, time-boxed engagement with specific scope and goals.

Types include black-box (no prior knowledge), white-box (full access), and gray-box (partial knowledge) testing. Regular pen testing is required for compliance standards like PCI-DSS and SOC 2.
